# Interaction Specification

- PowerButton starts/stops the boot sequence and reveals/hides the player panel.
- PlayButton or the right-side Play control starts the selected track; it does not use record clicks.
- PauseButton pauses with a smooth record slowdown and needle return.
- NextButton and album arrows/carousel gestures change the selected track.
- OrbitControls allow full azimuth rotation, with zoom and pan disabled.
- Record rotation, needle position, speaker pulse, screen state, and album label follow audio state.
