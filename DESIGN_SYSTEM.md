# Design System

- Canvas: bright neutral page with product-showcase whitespace.
- Housing: saturated cobalt/royal blue ABS-like materials.
- Accents: cyan display glow, gold hardware, dark rubber/black controls, cool brushed metal.
- UI: compact editorial music panel with blue emphasis, album carousel depth, hover/active states, and square transport icons.
- Motion: GSAP easing, subtle idle float/parallax, smooth carousel drag, progressive needle and record motion.
